# AI Chat

Give your chat a helper that never sleeps! Viewers ask a question and get a
friendly answer right away. You pick its personality, so it sounds just like
your stream.

## What it can do

- **Answer questions in chat.** Viewers type `!ask` and their question, and
  the AI answers them by name.
- **Answer when tagged.** Turn it on, and viewers can talk to your bot just by
  tagging its name, like `@YourBot what's up?`
- **Write lines for you.** Have the AI write a hype welcome for a raider, a
  thank-you for a new sub, or a fun fact to kick off your stream.
- **Stay on your vibe.** Tell it who to be: a pirate, a hype squad, a calm
  helper, or anything you like.

## Ideas to try

- When someone raids, have the AI write a big welcome for them and post it in
  chat.
- When a viewer redeems a channel point reward, have the AI roast their
  gameplay (nicely!).
- Let chat ask your bot about the game while you stay focused on playing.

## Getting started

1. Get an API key. You can use Claude from Anthropic
   (console.anthropic.com) or ChatGPT from OpenAI (platform.openai.com).
2. Open the AI Chat settings and paste your key.
3. Set **AI service** to `anthropic` for Claude, or `openai` for ChatGPT.
4. Write a short **Personality**, like "You are a cheerful space captain who
   loves puns."
5. Want @mentions? Turn on **Answer @mentions** and type your bot's chat name.

## Good to know

- The AI service charges your account a little for each answer. Short replies
  keep the cost low.
- Each viewer waits a bit between questions, so nobody can flood chat. You can
  change how long in the settings.
- Answers are kept short so they fit in chat. You can change the longest
  reply in the settings.
- The AI can make mistakes. Keep an eye on it, just like any other bot.
