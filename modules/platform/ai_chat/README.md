# AI Chat

Give your chat a helper that never sleeps. Viewers ask a question and get a friendly answer. You pick its personality, so it sounds like your stream.

## What you can do

- Answer a viewer's question when they type `!ask`
- Answer when someone tags your bot, like `@YourBot what's up?`
- Write a hype welcome for a raider or a thank-you for a new sub
- Pick who the AI is: a pirate, a hype squad, a calm helper

## Setting up

1. Get a key from an AI service: Claude (console.anthropic.com) or ChatGPT (platform.openai.com).
2. Open the AI Chat settings and paste your key.
3. Set **AI service** to `anthropic` for Claude or `openai` for ChatGPT.
4. Write a short **Personality**, like "You are a cheerful space captain who loves puns."
5. Want tags? Turn on **Answer @mentions** and type your bot's chat name.

The AI service charges your account a little for each answer. The AI can make mistakes, so keep an eye on it.

## Chat commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `!ask <question>` | The AI answers, tagging the viewer |
