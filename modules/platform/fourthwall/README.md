# Fourthwall

Make your merch part of the show! When a fan buys from your Fourthwall shop,
donates, or joins your membership, your stream can celebrate it live. Play a
sound, show an alert, or thank them in chat.

## What it can do

- **Donations.** Know the moment someone donates through your shop, with their
  name, amount and message.
- **Merch orders.** Cheer when someone buys your merch, and see what they
  picked.
- **Community gifts.** Celebrate when someone buys gifts to give away to your
  viewers.
- **New members.** Welcome people who join your membership.

## Ideas to try

- When someone orders merch, show a "new merch drop!" alert with their name.
- When someone buys community gifts, throw a big party on screen.
- When a new member joins, thank them by name in chat.

## Getting started

1. Install this module in WoofX3 and copy the webhook link it gives you.
2. On Fourthwall, go to **Settings → For developers**.
3. Add a new webhook, paste the link in, and pick the events you want:
   donations, orders, gift purchases and new memberships.
4. On the same page, copy your shop's **secret key**.
5. Paste the secret key into this module's settings in WoofX3.
6. Send a test event from Fourthwall to make sure it works.

## Good to know

Your stream only ever shows a fan's name and message. It never shows their
email.
