# Fourthwall

Make your merch part of the show. When a fan donates, buys from your Fourthwall shop, or joins your membership, your stream can celebrate it live.

## What you can do

- Someone donates: see their name, amount and message
- Someone orders merch: see what they picked
- Someone buys gifts for your community
- Someone joins your membership

## Setting up

1. Install this module in woofx3 and copy the link it gives you.
2. On Fourthwall, go to **Settings**, then **For developers**.
3. Add a new webhook and paste the link in. Pick the events you want: donations, orders, gift purchases and new memberships.
4. On the same page, copy your shop's secret key.
5. Paste the secret key into this module's settings in woofx3.
6. Send a test from Fourthwall to check it works.

Your stream only shows a fan's name and message, never their email.
