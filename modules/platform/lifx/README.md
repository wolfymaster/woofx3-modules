# LIFX

Make your room part of the show! Your LIFX lights can flash, glow and change
colour the moment something big happens on stream. Chat will see the hype, and
so will you.

## What it can do

- **Turn lights on or off.** Light up when you go live, and switch off when you
  end.
- **Set colour and brightness.** Pick any colour you like and how bright it
  should be. You can even fade slowly into the new look.
- **Flash for alerts.** Make your lights blink or gently breathe in a colour a
  few times, then go right back to how they were.

You can change every light at once, a whole room, or just one lamp. Just pick
from the list.

## Ideas to try

- When someone subscribes, flash your lights in your stream's colour.
- When someone raids, flash the whole room red.
- When a viewer redeems a channel point reward, slowly fade your lights to a
  new colour.
- When an ad break starts, dim the lights. When it ends, bring them back up.

## Getting started

1. Make sure your lights work in the LIFX app.
2. Go to **cloud.lifx.com** and sign in with your LIFX account.
3. Open **Settings**, then make a new **personal access token**. Copy it.
4. Paste it into the LIFX settings here.

## Good to know

- Your lights need to be powered on at the wall and online to change.
- LIFX only allows so many changes per minute. Flashing on every single chat
  message is too much, but alerts and rewards work great.
