# Throne

Celebrate every gift from your Throne wishlist, live on stream! When a fan
buys you a gift or chips in toward one, your stream can throw a party with
alerts, sounds and a big thank-you. Your supporters get the spotlight they
deserve, and you never miss a chance to say thanks.

## React to your supporters

Choose what your stream does when:

- **Someone buys you a gift.** Show what they bought, a picture of it, the
  price and their message.
- **Someone chips in toward a gift.** Show how much they gave and what it's
  going toward, along with their message.
- **A gift gets fully funded.** When your community teams up to pay for
  something together, celebrate the big moment!

If a supporter chooses to hide their name, they'll show up as "Anonymous." If
a gift is a surprise, your stream knows that too, so you can keep the secret.

## Ideas to try

- Show a thank-you alert with the gifter's name and message on screen.
- Post a thank-you in chat every time someone contributes.
- When a gift gets fully funded, switch to a celebration scene so everyone who
  helped can cheer together.

## Getting started

1. Install this module in WoofX3 and copy its webhook link.
2. In Throne, open the **webhook integration** settings.
3. Paste the link and save.

That's it! Only real messages from Throne can set off your alerts, so nobody
can fake a gift.
