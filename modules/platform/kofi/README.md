# Ko-fi

Turn every Ko-fi tip into a stream moment! When a fan supports you on Ko-fi,
your stream can cheer them on right away. Play a sound, show an alert, or say
thanks in chat, all on its own.

## What it can do

- **Tips.** Know the second someone sends you a tip, and see their name, how
  much they gave, and their message.
- **Memberships.** Celebrate new members and say thanks every time a member
  renews. You can even tell a brand-new member from a returning one.
- **Shop orders.** Cheer when someone buys something from your Ko-fi shop.
- **Commissions.** Get a heads-up when someone orders a commission.

## Ideas to try

- When someone tips, play a happy sound and show their message on screen.
- When a new member joins, give them a big welcome alert.
- When someone buys from your shop, thank them by name in chat.

## Getting started

1. Install this module in WoofX3 and copy the webhook link it gives you.
2. On Ko-fi, go to **Settings → API** (the webhooks page).
3. Paste the link into the **Webhook URL** box and save.
4. On that same page, open **Advanced** and copy your **Verification Token**.
5. Paste the token into this module's settings in WoofX3.
6. Back on Ko-fi, press **Send Test** to make sure it works.

## Good to know

Some fans ask Ko-fi to keep their tip private. When they do, your stream shows
them as "Anonymous" and keeps their message off screen. That way everyone's
choice is respected.
