# Ko-fi

Turn every Ko-fi tip into a stream moment. When a fan supports you on Ko-fi, your stream can cheer them on right away.

## What you can do

- Someone tips: see their name, amount and message
- Someone joins or renews a membership, and whether they're new
- Someone buys from your shop
- Someone orders a commission

## Setting up

1. Install this module in woofx3 and copy the link it gives you.
2. On Ko-fi, go to **Settings**, then **API**.
3. Paste the link into the **Webhook URL** box and save.
4. On the same page, open **Advanced** and copy your **Verification Token**.
5. Paste the token into this module's settings in woofx3.
6. Back on Ko-fi, press **Send Test** to check it works.

Some fans ask Ko-fi to keep their tip private. Your stream then shows them as "Anonymous" and keeps their message off screen.
