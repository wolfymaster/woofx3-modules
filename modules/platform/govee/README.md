# Govee

Light up your stream with Govee! Your lights can change colour, get brighter,
or switch on and off when big moments happen live. It's an easy way to make
your room feel as exciting as your chat.

## What it can do

- **Turn lights on or off.** Switch on when you go live, and off when you're
  done.
- **Change colour.** Pick any colour, like purple for a new follower or gold for
  a big cheer.
- **Change brightness.** Go bright for hype moments and dim for chill ones.

Just pick your light from the list. No typing names by hand!

## Ideas to try

- When someone follows, turn your light strip purple.
- When someone cheers bits, set your lights to gold.
- When a viewer redeems a channel point reward, switch your lights to a
  party colour.
- When you go live, turn on your lights and set them to full brightness.

## Getting started

1. Make sure your lights work in the Govee Home app.
2. In the Govee Home app, tap **Profile**, then **Settings** (the gear), then
   **Apply for API Key**.
3. Fill in the short form. Govee will email your key to you.
4. Paste the key into the Govee settings here.

## Good to know

- Your lights need to be powered on and online to change.
- Govee only allows so many changes per minute, so use your lights for big
  moments, not every chat message.
- Some older Govee lights can't be controlled this way. If a light isn't in
  the list, it isn't supported yet.
