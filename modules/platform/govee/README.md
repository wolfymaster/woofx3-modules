# Govee

Light up your stream. Your Govee lights can change color, get brighter, or switch on and off when big moments happen live.

## What you can do

- Turn a light on or off
- Change a light's color
- Change a light's brightness

Pick your light from a list. No typing names.

## Setting up

1. Make sure your lights work in the Govee Home app.
2. In the app, tap **Profile**, then **Settings** (the gear), then **Apply for API Key**.
3. Fill in the short form. Govee emails you your key.
4. Paste the key into this module's **Govee key** setting.

Lights must be on and online to change. Govee limits changes per minute, so save them for big moments. Some older lights can't be controlled this way; if yours isn't in the list, it isn't supported.
