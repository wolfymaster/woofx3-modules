# Kick

Make your stream react to everything that happens on Kick! When someone
follows, subs or sends KICKs, you can set off alerts, sounds, chat messages
and more. woofx3 can also do Kick jobs for you, like updating your stream
info or giving a rule breaker a timeout.

## What you can do

Pick a moment and choose what happens when it comes up.

**Support and celebrations**
- Someone follows
- Someone subscribes or resubscribes
- Someone gifts subs
- Someone sends KICKs

**Chat and rewards**
- Someone sends a chat message
- Someone redeems one of your channel rewards

**Your stream**
- You go live
- You go offline

Kick moments only start your Kick alerts, and Twitch moments only start your
Twitch alerts. Streaming on both at once? Set up an alert for each.

### Jobs woofx3 can do for you

- **Look up a streamer.** Find out who someone is, what they streamed last,
  and whether they're live. Use it in your next step.
- **Change your stream info.** Update your title, category or tags. Change just
  one, and the rest stay the same.
- **Time out a chatter.** Give a rule breaker a break from chat. Kick counts
  timeouts in whole minutes, from one minute up to seven days.

To post in your Kick chat, use the regular "Send chat message" step and pick
Kick.

## Ideas to try

- When someone sends KICKs, play a big sound and thank them in chat.
- When someone gifts subs, show an alert with how many they gave.
- When you go live, post a welcome message in your Kick chat.
- When someone redeems a reward, play the matching sound on stream.

## Good to know

- Kick has one sub level, so there is no tier to pick.
- Kick does not have raids, hype trains, clips or stream markers, so those
  moments and jobs are not here.

## Setting up

Connect your Kick account in your woofx3 dashboard first. Then install this
module, and you're ready to go!
