# Discord

Bring your community to your stream. This module tells your Discord when you go live, so friends and fans never miss a stream. You can also post to Discord whenever something big happens.

## What you can do

- Post in Discord when you go live, with a link to your stream
- Ping everyone, if you want
- Say thanks when your stream ends (off until you turn it on)
- Post any message when something happens on stream, with an optional card and link

## Setting up

1. Open Discord and go to your server.
2. Open **Server Settings**, then **Integrations**, then **Webhooks**.
3. Click **New Webhook** and pick the channel for your posts, like #going-live.
4. Click **Copy Webhook URL**.
5. Paste it into this module's **Discord channel link** setting.

Your next stream will be announced. In the settings you can change the go-live message (use {channel} for your channel name and {link} for your stream link), turn the @everyone ping on or off, and change the end-of-stream message.

Keep your Discord link private: anyone who has it can post in that channel. If it leaks, delete it in Discord and make a new one.
