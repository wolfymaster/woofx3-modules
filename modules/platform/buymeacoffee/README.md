# Buy Me a Coffee

Every coffee counts, so let your stream show it! When a fan buys you a coffee
or joins your membership, your stream can thank them live. Play a sound, pop
up an alert, or give them a shout in chat.

## What it can do

- **Coffees.** Know right away when someone buys you a coffee. See their name,
  how many coffees they bought, and their note.
- **New members.** Celebrate when someone joins one of your membership levels.
- **Cancelled memberships.** Find out when a member leaves, so you can keep
  track.

## Ideas to try

- When someone buys you a coffee, play a cozy sound and show their note on
  screen.
- When a new member joins, give them a big welcome alert with their level
  name.
- Thank every supporter by name in chat, so they feel seen.

## Getting started

1. Install this module in WoofX3 and copy the webhook link it gives you.
2. On Buy Me a Coffee, open **Integrations** and choose **Webhooks**.
3. Create a new webhook and paste the link in.
4. Pick the events you want, such as new supporters and new members.
5. Copy the **signing secret** Buy Me a Coffee shows you.
6. Paste the secret into this module's settings in WoofX3.
7. Send a test event from Buy Me a Coffee to make sure it works.

## Good to know

If a supporter hides their note, your stream keeps it hidden too.
