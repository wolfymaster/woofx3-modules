# Buy Me a Coffee

Every coffee counts, so let your stream show it. When a fan buys you a coffee or joins your membership, your stream can thank them live.

## What you can do

- Someone buys you a coffee: see their name, how many coffees, and their note
- Someone joins your membership: welcome them
- Someone cancels their membership: find out so you can keep track

## Setting up

1. Install this module in woofx3 and copy the link it gives you.
2. On Buy Me a Coffee, open **Integrations**, then **Webhooks**.
3. Create a new webhook and paste the link in.
4. Pick the events you want, like new supporters and new members.
5. Copy the secret Buy Me a Coffee shows you.
6. Paste the secret into this module's settings in woofx3.
7. Send a test from Buy Me a Coffee to check it works.

If a supporter hides their note, your stream keeps it hidden too.
