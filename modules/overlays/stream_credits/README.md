# Stream Credits

End your stream like a movie! Stream Credits rolls a thank-you to everyone who
made today's stream great. It shows who supported you, who raided you and who
chatted, one list at a time. Each list fades in, shines for a moment, then
fades out for the next. Everyone gets their name on screen, and chat loves
seeing that.

## What it can do

- **Thank your supporters.** Everyone who cheered bits or gifted subs gets a
  spot, with how much they gave right under their name.
- **Thank your raiders.** Every channel that raided you shows up, with how
  many viewers they brought.
- **Thank your chatters.** Everyone who said something in chat is listed,
  in ABC order.
- **Fade from list to list.** The lists take turns on a loop, so you can
  leave it up while you say goodbye.
- **Roll long lists.** A big list scrolls up slowly, just like real movie
  credits, so nobody gets cut off.
- **Skip empty lists.** No raids today? That list waits for next time.

## Getting started

1. Install the Twitch module first, so the credits hear about cheers, gift
   subs, raids and chat.
2. Add Stream Credits to your "ending" scene.
3. Pick your titles, colours and size. Add a headline like "Thanks for
   watching!" if you want one.
4. Stream as usual. The names fill in by themselves.

## Make it yours

- Rename each list. Call your supporters "Legends" or your chatters "The
  Pack". It's up to you.
- Turn any list on or off.
- Show or hide the bits, gift subs and raid sizes under each name.
- Choose how long each list stays up and how fast long lists scroll.
- Pick your accent colour and text colour, and turn the dark panel behind
  the credits on or off.

## Good to know

- The credits start fresh every stream.
- Your bots stay out of the chatters list. Nightbot, StreamElements and other
  common bots are left out from the start, and you can add more names in the
  settings.
- You're not listed as a chatter in your own credits. It's your show!
- **!resetcredits** clears all three lists. Only you and your moderators can
  use it.
- Anonymous cheers and gifts are thanked as "Anonymous".
