# Hype Board

Put your biggest supporters in the spotlight! The Hype Board shows who
cheered the most bits, who gifted the most subs and who tipped the most, right
on your stream. It can also run a subathon timer that grows with every sub,
cheer and tip, and track a goal with rewards along the way. Chat sees every
bit of support count, and that keeps the hype rolling.

## What it can do

- **Crown your top supporters.** Your top cheerer, top gifter and top tipper
  each get their own spot. When someone takes the lead, their name pops up
  right away.
- **Run a subathon timer.** Every sub, cheer and tip adds time to the clock.
  You choose how much time each one is worth.
- **Track a goal.** Pick subs, bits or tips, set a number, and watch the
  count climb.
- **Show milestones.** List rewards like "Movie night at 500 subs". Each one
  lights up when chat unlocks it.
- **Add up the totals.** See how many subs, bits and tips came in.
- **Add a note.** Put a short line of your own on the board, like your
  birthday or your schedule.

Tips count from Ko-fi, Throne, Buy Me a Coffee and Fourthwall.

## Pick a look

The Hype Board comes with three themes. Each one shows the parts that suit it
best, and you can turn any part on or off.

- **Boss Battle.** Cozy autumn badges crown your Bit Boss, Gift Boss and
  Tip Boss.
- **Cotton Candy.** Dreamy pastel cards with sparkles. It's made for a
  subathon timer, a goal and milestones.
- **Arcade.** A sleek scoreboard strip with a glowing timer, a goal bar and
  the high scores. It fits nicely along the top or bottom of your screen.

## Getting started

1. Install the Twitch module first, so the board hears about cheers and subs.
   Install Ko-fi, Throne, Buy Me a Coffee or Fourthwall too if you take tips
   there.
2. Add the Hype Board to a scene and pick a theme.
3. Set your goal and add any milestones you want.
4. Show or hide parts until the board looks just right.

## Subathon timer

Start the timer from chat, then let your community keep it going!

The Hype Board makes a timer for you called **Hype Board subathon**. You'll
find it with your other timers. It's a regular timer, so you can also show it,
start it or pause it anywhere else you use timers. Want to use a timer you
already have? Pick it in the Hype Board settings instead.

- **!subathon** tells anyone in chat how much time is left.
- **!subathon start 4h** starts the timer. Leave off the time to start from
  the timer's own length. You set that length on the timer itself.
- **!subathon add 10m** and **!subathon remove 10m** change the time.
- **!subathon pause** and **!subathon resume** stop and restart the clock.
- **!subathon end** ends the subathon.

Only you and your moderators can change the timer. Times can be written like
4h, 90m, 1h30m or 45s.

In the settings you can choose:

- How much time a sub, 100 bits and each 1 of your currency in tips add.
  Tier 2 subs add twice as much, and tier 3 subs add five times as much.
- The most time the timer can hold, so it can't run forever.
- Whether the timer pauses while you're offline. It's on to start with, so
  a subathon can last for days.

## Good to know

- Totals and top supporters start fresh every stream. Want them to keep
  counting across streams, like for a month-long event? Turn on **Keep totals
  between streams**.
- **!resetboard** sets the totals and top supporters back to zero. Only you
  and your moderators can use it. It doesn't touch the timer.
- Gift subs count for the person who gifted them, so a gift bomb crowns its
  gifter.
- Once the timer runs out, the subathon is over and new support adds no more
  time. A moderator can still add time to bring it back.
- Tips are added up in one currency. Set your currency symbol in the
  settings.
