# Wolfy Profile

Wolfy's own alert pack, ready to drop into your stream! Every follow, sub, gift
and cheer gets a fun, loud celebration on screen, with the viewer's name in big
letters so they know you saw them.

## What's inside

- **New follower.** A playful "I see you!" video pops up with the follower's
  name and a sound to match.
- **New sub.** A happy husky celebrates the new subscriber, backed by a wolf
  hype sound.
- **Gifted subs.** A "money, money, money" video shows who gifted and how many
  subs they gave, with a feel-good song.
- **Bits.** A bright Bits animation shows how many Bits came in, with confetti
  raining down and a party song.

## Ideas to try

- Use this pack as-is to get great alerts on your stream in minutes.
- Use it as a starting point, then swap in your own videos, sounds and words to
  make it yours.

## Getting started

Install the pack and add the alert to your OBS scene. Your alerts work with
follows, subs, gifts and cheers from Twitch, so link your Twitch account to
WoofX3 too.
