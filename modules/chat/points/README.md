# Loyalty Points

Reward the people who show up! Your viewers earn points just by chatting.
Then they can show them off, share them with friends, or risk them all on a
coin flip. It gives chat a reason to stick around and a fun game to play
together.

## What it can do

- **Earn points by chatting.** Every time a viewer chats, they earn points.
  Subscribers earn extra. Spamming doesn't help, because each viewer can only
  earn once in a short window.
- **Name your currency.** Call your points anything you like, such as bones,
  coins or cookies.
- **Give out bonuses.** Hand out points when someone follows, subs, raids or
  cheers. You pick the amount.
- **Show a leaderboard.** Chat can see who has the most points.
- **Let viewers gamble.** Viewers can bet their points on a coin flip. Win,
  and they double their bet. Lose, and it's gone!

## Chat commands

Anyone in chat can use these:

- **!points** shows how many points you have. Add a name to check someone
  else, like **!points wolfy**. You can also type **!** plus your currency
  name, like **!bones**.
- **!give wolfy 50** gives 50 of your points to wolfy.
- **!gamble 100** bets 100 points. You can also bet **half** or **all**.
- **!leaderboard** or **!top** shows the top five.

Only you and your moderators can use these:

- **!addpoints wolfy 500** gives wolfy 500 points.
- **!removepoints wolfy 500** takes 500 points away from wolfy.

## Ideas to try

- Give 100 points to every new follower.
- Give 500 points to anyone who raids you.
- Give a big bonus to anyone who gifts subs.
- Cheer on a big win: when someone's points jump, show an alert on screen.

## Getting started

1. Make sure your Twitch channel is connected to WoofX3.
2. Pick a name for your points and how many viewers earn per message.
3. Decide if you want gambling on, and how long viewers wait between bets.

That's it! Points start adding up as soon as your chat starts talking.

## Good to know

- A viewer who has never chatted can still get bonus points, such as for a
  follow. Their points wait for them the first time they say hi in chat.
- Points can't go below zero.
