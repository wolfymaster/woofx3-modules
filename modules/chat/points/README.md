# Loyalty Points

Reward the people who show up. Viewers earn your own currency just by chatting, then share it, gamble it or climb the leaderboard.

## What you can do

- Give points for chatting, with a bonus for subscribers
- Name your currency anything you like: bones, coins, cookies
- Hand out bonus points when someone follows, subs, raids or cheers
- Show a leaderboard
- Let viewers bet points on a coin flip
- Show an alert when someone's points jump

## Setting up

1. Connect your Twitch channel to woofx3.
2. Open the Loyalty Points settings and pick a name for your points.
3. Choose how many points viewers earn per message and whether gambling is on.

## Chat commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| !points | Shows your points. Add a name to check someone else, like `!points wolfy`. `!` plus your currency name works too, like `!bones` |
| !give wolfy 50 | Gives 50 of your points to wolfy |
| !gamble 100 | Bets 100 points. You can also bet `half` or `all` |
| !leaderboard or !top | Shows the top five |
| !addpoints wolfy 500 | Gives wolfy 500 points (mods and you only) |
| !removepoints wolfy 500 | Takes 500 points from wolfy (mods and you only) |

Viewers who have never chatted can still get bonus points. They're waiting for them the first time they say hi. Points never go below zero.
