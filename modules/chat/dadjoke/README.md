# Dad Jokes

Bring the groans! With this module, anyone in chat can type **!dadjoke** and
get a random dad joke. It's a quick way to fill a slow moment, break the ice
with new viewers, or make your whole chat roll their eyes at once. No setup
needed!

## What your chat can do

- **!dadjoke** tells a random dad joke right in chat.

There are 500 jokes built in. Jokes that were told recently get skipped, so
your chat won't hear the same one over and over. Puns, knock-knock jokes,
"I used to be a..." jokes and a few about streaming are all in the mix.

## Ideas to try

- Tell a dad joke while you wait in a game lobby or between matches.
- Ask chat to type !dadjoke when the stream needs a little pick-me-up.
- Give a shout-out to whoever finds the worst joke of the night.

## Good to know

- Every joke is family-friendly, so it's safe for any stream.
- If another module already has its own !dadjoke, keep just one so chat
  doesn't get two answers.
