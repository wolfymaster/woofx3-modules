# Timed Messages

Keep your chat in the loop without lifting a finger! Timed Messages posts
friendly reminders in chat while you stream: your Discord, your socials, your
schedule, or anything else you want viewers to know.

## What it can do

- **Post your messages on a timer.** Pick how many minutes to wait between
  them.
- **Take turns.** Add up to five messages. They go out one after another, so
  chat sees something fresh each time.
- **Wait for chat to be active.** A message only goes out after viewers have
  been chatting. A quiet chat never fills up with reminders nobody reads.
- **Only post while you are live.** Your chat stays calm when you are offline.

## Ideas to try

- "Join our Discord to hang out between streams!"
- "Enjoying the stream? Hit that follow button so you know when I go live!"
- "I stream Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 7pm."
- "Got a song you love? Request it with !sr."

## Getting started

1. Open the Timed Messages settings.
2. Type your messages into the message boxes. Leave any box empty if you
   don't need it.
3. Choose how many minutes to wait between messages. 15 is a great start.
4. Choose how many chat messages viewers should send in between. 5 works well
   for most streams. Pick 0 to post on the timer no matter what.

Your messages start the next time you go live.

## Good to know

- After each message, the timer starts over. Chat also needs to be active
  again before the next message goes out.
- Want messages even when you are offline? Turn off **Only while live**.
