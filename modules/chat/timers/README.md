# Timed Messages

Keep chat in the loop without lifting a finger. Post friendly reminders while you stream: your Discord, your socials, your schedule.

## What you can do

- Post up to five messages, one after another
- Choose how many minutes to wait between messages
- Wait for chat to be active, so a quiet chat never fills up with reminders
- Post only while you're live

## Setting up

1. Open the Timed Messages settings.
2. Type your messages into the message boxes. Leave any empty that you don't need.
3. Choose how many minutes to wait between messages. 15 is a great start.
4. Choose how much viewers should chat in between. 5 works for most streams. Pick 0 to post on the timer alone.

Your messages start the next time you go live.

## Ideas to try

- "Join our Discord to hang out between streams!"
- "Enjoying the stream? Hit follow so you know when I go live!"
- "I stream Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 7pm."

Want messages while you're offline? Turn off **Only while live**.
