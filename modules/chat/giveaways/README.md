# Giveaways

Throw a giveaway right in your chat! Viewers type one word to enter, and a
winner is picked at random with one command. It's quick, it's fair, and chat
loves the suspense.

## What it can do

- **Start a giveaway from chat.** You or your mods type `!giveaway start`.
- **Let viewers enter with one word.** Viewers type `!join` to get in. Everyone
  can only enter once.
- **Pick a winner.** Type `!giveaway draw` and a random winner is named in
  chat. Draw again to pick someone new.
- **Reward your subscribers.** Give subscribers extra luck if you want to.
- **Celebrate on screen.** Show an alert when a giveaway starts, when someone
  joins, or when a winner is picked.

## Commands for you and your mods

- `!giveaway start` opens the giveaway. Viewers enter with `!join`.
- `!giveaway start !pizza` opens it with your own entry word, like `!pizza`.
- `!giveaway end` stops new entries but keeps everyone who joined.
- `!giveaway draw` stops new entries and picks a winner.
- `!giveaway cancel` throws the giveaway away.
- `!giveaway` tells chat how the giveaway is going.

Only you and your moderators can use these. Viewers only need `!join`.

## Ideas to try

- Start a giveaway when a viewer redeems a special channel point reward.
- Play a drumroll and a big celebration when the winner is picked.
- Give subscribers two chances to win as a thank you for their support.

## Getting started

1. Open the Giveaways settings.
2. Choose your entry word. `!join` works great.
3. Set **Subscriber luck**. Leave it at 1 to give everyone the same chance, or
   pick 2 to give subscribers twice the chance.
4. Type `!giveaway start` in chat, and you're off!

## Good to know

- One giveaway runs at a time.
- Turn on **Say when someone enters** to thank each viewer in chat. In a busy
  chat, leave it off to keep things tidy.
